Privacy Policy／隱私權政策

Effective: November 8, 2026

SPIDER WEB Backup is a personal-use Google Drive encrypted backup integration under development for a read-only market observer. This policy describes its intended data handling. It is not offered as a public account-based service. Live Google Drive transfer and independent restoration have not yet been validated.

What Google data is accessed

The application requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file to create, access and manage application-authorized backup files in the owner's Google Drive. Before an authorized backup, it is designed to query Google Drive account identity and quota information (including an account permission identifier, quota limit and used storage). It does not seek permission to browse unrelated Drive files, Gmail, contacts or calendars.

How the data is used

Account and quota data are used to confirm the intended storage account and prevent unsafe transfers. The SQLite snapshot is intended to be encrypted locally with restic before storage in Google Drive through rclone. The public pages are informational only: they have no OAuth sign-in form, no embedded analytics, and no code for collecting Google tokens or file contents.

Where data is held and who can access it

Private OAuth configuration and restic encryption passwords are intended to remain on devices or systems controlled by the owner, never in this public repository or website. Encrypted backup objects, if the owner separately authorizes production transfer, are stored in the owner's chosen Google Drive account. Website hosting by GitHub and cloud storage by Google are subject to their own infrastructure logging and service policies.

Sharing and advertising

SPIDER WEB Backup does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, targeting or AI model training. Access to backup ciphertext is limited to the owner-authorized storage service and the service provider processing necessary to store or retrieve it. This website contains no third-party analytics trackers.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Google access can be revoked from the owner's Google Account settings. Revoking access prevents subsequent authorized operations but does not itself delete previously uploaded backups. The owner may separately manage or delete backup files in their Drive account. This integration is designed to avoid unattended deletion, prune or overwrite of backups. Restoration and data retention must be verified by the owner before any destructive action.

Changes and questions

Any future change to the application's actual data handling should be reflected in this policy. Questions can be raised through the public GitHub issue tracker; do not post private information, tokens, passwords, account identifiers or backup contents.
